Case Study: 340% Traffic Increase for a Legal Firm

📅 Feb 18, 2025
⏱ 5 min read
✍️ Divyam Jain

This is the breakdown of how I took a Mumbai-based legal services firm from 800 monthly organic visitors to over 3,500 in five months — without any paid advertising, without building hundreds of backlinks, and without creating a single piece of clickbait content.

The client name is confidential, but every number here is real.

Where They Started

The firm had a 6-year-old WordPress site that hadn't been meaningfully updated since 2021. It was slow (PageSpeed 34 on mobile), had no structured SEO strategy, and was ranking for their brand name and essentially nothing else. They were getting referral traffic and word-of-mouth but virtually zero organic search traffic.

Their competitors — some larger, some the same size — were dominating the first page for every keyword their potential clients were searching.

What I Did — Month by Month

Month 1
Technical audit and site rebuild

Full technical SEO audit. Rebuilt the site in a clean WordPress theme with proper semantic HTML, schema markup (Organisation + LocalBusiness + Service), and fixed all 47 crawl errors flagged in Search Console. PageSpeed went from 34 to 91 on mobile.

Month 2
Keyword strategy and on-page optimisation

Mapped 38 target keywords across 12 service pages. Rewrote every title tag and meta description. Restructured page content around search intent. Added FAQ sections with FAQPage schema to 6 pages targeting question-based searches.

Month 3
Content cluster launch

Published 4 long-form blog posts targeting informational keywords around their practice areas. Built internal links from these posts to the relevant service pages. Set up Google Business Profile properly for local SEO.

Month 4
Rankings begin moving

First significant ranking movements. 11 keywords moved onto page 1. Organic traffic crossed 1,800 monthly visitors. Published 4 more blog posts. Added 3 client testimonials with structured markup to the homepage.

Month 5
3,500+ visitors, 3 new client enquiries per week

Traffic hit 3,500+ and kept climbing. 28 keywords on page 1. The firm started receiving 3 qualified online enquiries per week from organic search — versus near zero before I started. SEO retainer renewed for the next 6 months.

The Three Things That Drove Most of the Growth

  • The technical rebuild. A PageSpeed score of 34 was actively suppressing every page on the site. Google doesn't rank pages it can't crawl and render quickly. Fixing this was the foundation for everything else.
  • Matching content to search intent. Their service pages were written for existing clients who already knew the firm — not for people searching "commercial property lawyer Mumbai" who had never heard of them. Rewriting for the searcher, not the client, was the biggest content shift.
  • Consistency. Eight blog posts over three months isn't a lot, but it was eight more than they'd published in the previous three years. Google rewards sites that publish regularly. The consistency signal alone accelerated indexing and crawling of the whole site.

The honest takeaway: This wasn't magic. It was a broken technical foundation repaired, content aligned to what people actually search, and a consistent publishing schedule maintained.
