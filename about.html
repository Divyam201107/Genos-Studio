My Story

I Am Divyam Jain.

I build websites, SEO strategies, and content for businesses that are serious about growing online. No fluff. No bloated retainers. Just results.

I got tired of watching great businesses get buried by mediocre ones.

Work With Me

Genos Studio is me — Divyam Jain. It was born out of frustration. I kept seeing brilliant businesses — real products, real value, real passion — getting outranked by competitors who had nothing but a better web presence. That felt wrong.

So I built a practice that does three things properly: web design that converts, SEO that sticks, and content that compounds. Not three separate services bolted together, but one connected system designed to get you found, make you trusted, and turn visitors into clients.

Every project I take on gets my full attention. I stay selective on purpose. I'd rather do fewer things exceptionally well than take on every project and do mediocre work for everyone.

What I Stand For

Built on Principles.

01
Honesty Over Hype

I don't promise page-one rankings in 30 days. I tell you what's realistic, what's possible, and what it actually takes. That's rarer than it should be.

02
Systems, Not Sprints

I build things that compound. A well-built website, a growing backlink profile, a content library — these get more valuable over time. I think in years, not campaigns.

03
Your Growth is My Scorecard

I don't measure success in deliverables. I measure it in your traffic, your leads, your revenue. If you're not growing, something needs to change, and I'll say so.

Why I'm Different

The Honest Difference.

01 —
I'm not an agency

No account managers, no junior staff handling your work. When you work with me, you work directly with the person doing the work. That means faster decisions, better quality, and zero telephone-game errors.

02 —
Design + SEO as one system

Most studios hand off a beautiful website with zero thought for how Google will index it. Most SEO agencies optimise pages that look terrible. I design for users and search engines simultaneously — the result is a site that ranks and converts.

03 —
I say no to the wrong projects

If your business isn't a good fit for what I do, I'll tell you. I'd rather point you in the right direction than take your money and underdeliver. It keeps my reputation intact and means every client I do take on gets my best work.
